# The angle: « Mode avion »

**Propagande is the only clipper that keeps working when you cut the internet.**

Opus Clip, Vizard and Higgsfield Katana are cloud services: no connection, no clips, and every minute of video costs them servers, so they sell credits. Propagande runs on the user's machine. After the first visit (the voice model downloads once), the site opens and cuts with no connection at all. Tested 2026-10-20: a run with the model stored makes no request outside our own site, and the page reloads from the device with the server shut down.

## Why this angle beats the others
- **One image proves three promises.** Wi-Fi off and clips coming out means it's free (no server to pay), private (nothing can leave) and unlimited. Each claim alone sounds like marketing; the demo makes them facts.
- **Nobody has shown it.** Every AI video demo needs the cloud. A clip tool in airplane mode is a first-frame surprise.
- **A cloud company can't copy it** without rebuilding its product and giving up credits, its business model.

## Who it hits hardest
1. **Clippers paid per view.** Volume is their income: unlimited clips at 0 $ goes straight to their margin. Line: « Illimité. Ça ne nous coûte rien, alors ça ne te coûte rien. »
2. **Anyone holding footage they can't upload:** agencies under NDA, unreleased podcast episodes, client footage, films before release (studios are among the biggest clipping buyers). With a cloud tool, the footage sits on someone else's servers; here it can't leave. Line: « Ta vidéo ne quitte jamais ton ordi. Elle ne peut pas : il n'y a pas de serveur. »
3. **Québec creators.** Québec French is understood and the language is detected on its own; generic tools stumble on it.

## The demo (the P2 long video and the launch clip)
1. Frame 0: the Wi-Fi icon goes off (screen recording, nothing faked).
2. Drop a 30-minute video. A timer runs.
3. 8 clips come out: captions, the spoken word lit, punch-ins, openings.
4. Close: « Mode avion. 30 minutes. 8 clips. 0 $. » Then the link.

Say once, plainly: the first visit downloads the voice model (566 MB) with internet; after that, no connection is needed.

## Lines to use (plain, no teaser, no emoji)
- « Coupe ton Wi-Fi. Propagande coupe quand même. »
- « Tes clips se font sur ton ordi. Pas de serveur, pas de crédits, pas de limite. »
- « Ta vidéo ne quitte jamais ton ordi. »

## Don't claim
- That it works offline on the very first visit (the model must download once).
- That it's faster than everyone: speed depends on the machine. Use the measured numbers only (6 min → 87 s, 30 min → under 7 min on a recent Mac).
